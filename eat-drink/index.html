The Z Café

Hostel vibe. Restaurant-quality food.

The Z Café houses the Original Z blend coffee, a buffet breakfast, and sumptuous bestsellers such as Adobo Flakes, Sisig and Beef Sinigang. New additions like Yakitori and Street Ramen take hostel dining to a whole new level. Alcoholic beverages and refreshments are always available during café hours.

The Z Roofdeck

Now world-renowned, the Z Roofdeck has put the hostel on a sweet, top spot in the traveling industry. A fantastic view of the Manila skyline from every angle, a bar serving cold beers, fresh cocktails and cracking munchies, and a DJ deck that has become home to local and international artists.
