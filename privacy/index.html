Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated October 15, 2026

Flashpacker Inc. (Z Hostel), 5660 Don Pedro St., Brgy. Poblacion, Makati City, is the personal information controller for data collected on this website, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, and optionally your phone number, company, and answers to optional event questions (for example how many friends you're bringing, how you like to pay, your age bracket). We also record which link brought you here (for example an Instagram story) so we know which channels work.

Why

To answer your inquiry, put you on an event list, plan events (bar, stages, staffing, safety), send you the sponsorship deck if you asked for it, and, only if you ticked the box, email you about future Z events and offers.

Who sees it

Z Hostel staff only. The data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) and is never sold. The public website cannot read it.

How long

Event and inquiry records are deleted after about 13 months unless you opted in to marketing emails. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by emailing marketing@zhostel.com.
