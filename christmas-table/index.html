Good to know

Why are prices shown as ranges?

Your final price depends on the menu you settle on, the date and your headcount. We quote within the range you see, and a 50% downpayment locks it.

Can I start with just ₱1,000?

Yes, if your date is 45 days away or more. It pencils your date for 7 days while we plan with you. As your date gets closer, a bigger downpayment is needed to keep it.

What if our plans change?

You can move your date once, free of charge, as long as the new date is open. Your downpayment carries over and is always credited to your bill.

When is the final headcount due?

5 days before your party. We bill the confirmed headcount.
