About Z

Bringing people together since 2015.

The basics

A new breed of hostel.

Z Hostel combines a higher standard of accommodation with a fun, social atmosphere. Every detail in each room was thought through for the guest. The Café gives you a taste of authentic Filipino cuisine, while the Roofdeck offers great entertainment nightly from local and international artists and DJs.

The managing owners are a group of friends who took their time to conceptualize and build the hostel from an inspiration: their Sunday Slowdown sessions at a beach resort called La Luz. A certain vibe was born there from music, like-minded people, strangers becoming lifelong friends, and good conversation. That was the foundation of Z Hostel.

The same foundation kept Z going through the pandemic. Z opened in 2015 at 5660 Don Pedro St., and is back in full swing, stronger than ever, with the same brand of good vibes and top-notch service.

Our purpose

Z Street is a place of belonging for everyone. It keeps Poblacion's streets alive and safe so people can be themselves.

2017

Z Street begins

Two years after opening, Z Hostel's founders closed Don Pedro and Alfonso Streets for the first Z Street block party. Since then: 25 parties and counting.

Partners

Co-organized with PLUD

Every Z Street is co-organized with Poblacion Land & Urban Development Corp. and fully permitted by the City of Makati and Barangay Poblacion. In 2023 the Makati Tourism Office formally endorsed the event calendar.

A first

RFID payments

Z introduced the first RFID payment system in a hostel in the Philippines: cashless tabs at the bar, faster service, no waiting for change. It later powered the cashless wristbands at Zombie Street 2019.

Explore the hood

Right in the center of a business and cultural district.

Dotted with hole-in-the-wall restaurants, quaint coffee shops and art spaces, Z Hostel's neighborhood is a throbbing heartbeat in the middle of a bustling city. Join our Sunset Sessions from 6 p.m. at the Roofdeck (one free cocktail for in-house guests), then get walking and sample local and international food all around.
