Halloween history

The night keeps growing.

From the first takeover of Don Pedro in 2017 to the biggest Halloween street party in Manila. Year by year.

2017

Z Street Halloween begins

The first Halloween takeover of Don Pedro.

2018

The word gets out

The crowd grows fast, and Halloween becomes the night Poblacion plans its year around.

2019

Zombie Street at Poblacion

Venues along Don Pedro included Z Hostel Roofdeck, The Apartment, The Ruins and The Green Door. A cashless RFID wristband system ran across the participating bars. Covered by Primer and The Scoop.

2020–21

Paused for the pandemic

Z kept the music going online with the Z Hostel Lockdown Sessions: live DJ and acoustic sets streamed from the Roofdeck.

2022

Ready Poblacion One

The comeback: a video-game-themed party powered by Budweiser, with two stages and twelve DJs. Covered by Manila Bulletin, VillagePipol and MNL Concert Scene.

2023

Haunted Hallow Bash

A Day-of-the-Dead theme with sugar-skull streets, and no major medical incidents.

2024

Red Nights Halloween / Halloween Invasion

Our biggest crowd yet. Traveloka listed it as one of the biggest Halloween parties in Makati.

2025

Halloween Shadow Realm

A Poblacion street party with a gothic red-curtain gate across Don Pedro, Alfonso and Fermina Streets. Billed by When In Manila as Manila's biggest Halloween street party.

2026

THE ODY-Z

A Poblacion homecoming street party, moved to Friday, November 20.

Why Z Street is different

The biggest party in the city, run by people who know what they're doing.

The original.

We've thrown Poblacion street parties since 2017, longer than anyone, and from the street we live on.

Fully permitted.

Every event is cleared with Makati City Hall, Barangay Poblacion, the Bureau of Fire Protection, PNP, the Public Safety Department, MDRRMO, the Makati Health Department and PDEA. The calendar is endorsed by the Makati Tourism Office.

Safety first.

Professional crowd management (Red Knights, our partner since 2019), K-9 units, CCTV across both streets, EMT stations, ambulances, a fire truck on standby, a command center, and hospital coordination with Makati Med, St. Luke's and Ospital ng Makati. Strict house rules and no alcohol for minors.

Good for the neighborhood.

Z Street fills Poblacion's bars, restaurants and small businesses, and puts the district on Manila's tourism map. Dozens of local venues take part every year.

Party guide

Before you go

Where is it?

On Don Pedro St. and Alfonso St., Poblacion, Makati. The gates are at Kalayaan Ave./Jacobo St. and Fermina St. Z Hostel is at 5660 Don Pedro St., right on the party street.

What time does it start and end?

Usually 5–6 p.m. until 1–3 a.m. To confirm: hours per event

Is it free or ticketed?

To confirm: free or ticketed, per event

Is there an age limit?

To confirm: age limit (18+?)

What can't I bring?

No weapons, no dangerous items and no drugs. Organizers may refuse entry. There's no alcohol for minors.

Is there a costume contest?

Yes. Halloween on Z Street always has costume contests and themed gates. Go full character.

Can I stay nearby?

Yes. Z Hostel is on the street. Event weekends sell out, so book early.

I lost something. Who do I contact?

Message the Z Hostel front desk on (63) 917 889 8531 or send us a note with what you lost and where.

How do I get there, and where do I park?

Poblacion is walkable from the Makati CBD; take a ride-hail drop-off at Kalayaan Ave. or Fermina St. Streets around the party are closed on the night, so parking is very limited. Directions to Z (PDF).
