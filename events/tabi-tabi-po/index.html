Ask before
you pass.

Growing up, we were told to whisper “tabi tabi po” when walking past an anthill, an old balete tree, or a dark field. Excuse me, please let me through. Somebody you can't see might live there.

This Halloween, Salakot opens its doors at Z Hostel and lets them all in. The tikbalang, the kapre, the lady in white. DJs till late, drinks at the bar, and a costume contest for whoever brings the best spirit from home.

  • DJ sets all night
  • Best in costume prize
  • Salakot bar opening
  • Poblacion, Makati

Come as
a Filipino myth.

Best in costume goes to the guest who brings folklore to life. Need an idea? Start with the ones your lola warned you about.

  • Tikbalang

    Horse-headed trickster of the forest who leads travelers in circles.

  • Kapre

    Giant who sits in old trees, smoking a cigar that never burns out.

  • Manananggal

    Leaves her lower half behind at night and flies off on bat wings.

  • Aswang

    Shape-shifter of the provinces. Friendly neighbor by day.

  • Diwata

    Guardian spirit of mountains and forests. Think Maria Makiling.

  • Nuno sa Punso

    Old dwarf of the anthill. The reason we say “tabi tabi po.”

  • Tiyanak

    Cries like a lost baby in the woods. Don't pick it up.

  • White Lady

    Balete Drive's most famous late-night passenger.
